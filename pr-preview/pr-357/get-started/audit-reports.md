## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem. Report it privately by email to [rollups@cartesi.foundation](mailto:rollups@cartesi.foundation).

Please include:

- The affected component and version (release tag or commit).
- The impact and who is affected.
- Steps to reproduce, ideally a script or a test.

Reports sent by email are not encrypted. If your report contains sensitive material, mention it in your first message.

### What happens next

We review each report, decide how to handle it, and reply with the outcome. When a fix is released and the issue affects users, we may describe it on this page. Please give us a reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.
